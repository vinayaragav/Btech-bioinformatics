Module 03

Real-World Applications

A look at current and emerging uses of CRISPR technology.

💚

Gene therapy

Casgevy (exagamglogene autotemcel) is an approved CRISPR-based treatment for sickle cell disease and transfusion-dependent beta-thalassemia in eligible patients. It edits a patient's blood stem cells outside the body.

🌱

Agricultural research

Researchers are investigating ways to improve crop traits such as disease resistance, yield, and tolerance to environmental stress. Outcomes and regulatory treatment vary.

🔬

Rapid diagnostics

CRISPR-based systems such as SHERLOCK are being developed and studied for detecting nucleic-acid targets, including those from pathogens.
